# ShareBridge

## Purpose

ShareBridge is a location-based community donation platform designed to connect people who want to donate with volunteers who can help deliver donations to individuals, families, and communities in need.

The platform aims to reduce wasted resources by making it easier to distribute food, supplies, clothing, and other essential items through a trusted and transparent network.

ShareBridge combines donation management, volunteer coordination, social networking, and reputation systems into a single platform.

---

# Problem Being Solved

Many people are willing to donate food and supplies but often face challenges such as:

* Not knowing where to donate.
* Not having transportation to deliver donations.
* Lack of trusted volunteers.
* Difficulty verifying legitimate donors and volunteers.
* Poor communication between participants.

ShareBridge addresses these issues by creating a centralized platform where donations can be posted, volunteers can assist with deliveries, and users can build trust through verified interactions and ratings.

---

# User Roles

## Donator

Users who donate food, supplies, clothing, medicine, or other resources.

Capabilities:

* Create donation posts.
* Upload photos of donated items.
* Manage donation availability.
* Coordinate with volunteers.
* Send and receive messages.
* Rate volunteers after completed deliveries.
* Track donation history.

---

## Volunteer

Users who help transport and deliver donations.

Capabilities:

* Browse available donations.
* Accept delivery requests.
* View pickup and destination locations.
* Communicate with donors and recipients.
* Update delivery progress.
* Build reputation through ratings.
* Track volunteer history.

---

## Admin

Platform administrators responsible for maintaining system integrity.

Capabilities:

* Manage users.
* Moderate content.
* Handle reports.
* Verify users when necessary.
* Review platform activity.
* Manage system-wide operations.

# Core Features

## Donation Posts

Users can create donation listings containing:

* Title
* Description
* Category
* Quantity
* Photos
* Pickup location
* Availability status

Examples:

* Food
* Water
* Clothing
* School supplies
* Hygiene kits
* Medical supplies

---

## Real-Time Location System

The platform provides location-aware functionality.

Features:

* Donation pickup locations.
* Delivery destinations.
* Volunteer navigation support.
* Nearby donation discovery.
* Delivery tracking.

---

## Volunteer Delivery Workflow

A complete donation delivery lifecycle.

Typical flow:

1. Donator creates donation post.
2. Volunteer discovers donation.
3. Volunteer accepts delivery.
4. Donator and volunteer communicate.
5. Donation is picked up.
6. Donation is delivered.
7. Participants rate each other.

---

## Social Media Feed

ShareBridge includes a social platform to encourage community engagement.

Features:

* Public posts.
* Community updates.
* Donation stories.
* Volunteer activities.
* Feed system.
* Comments.
* Reactions.
* User profiles.

The experience should feel familiar to users of social media platforms while remaining focused on community service.

---

## Direct Messaging

Private communication between users.

Supported conversations:

* Donator ↔ Volunteer
* Donator ↔ Donator
* Volunteer ↔ Volunteer
* Admin ↔ Users

Features:

* Private chats.
* Conversation history.
* Message status.
* Real-time communication.

---

## Rating and Reputation System

Trust and legitimacy are critical to the platform.

Users can rate one another after completed interactions.

Ratings help determine:

* Reliability
* Trustworthiness
* Community reputation

Profile pages display:

* Average rating
* Rating count
* Volunteer history
* Donation history

---

## User Profiles

Every user has a public profile containing:

* Basic information
* Profile photo
* Ratings
* Activity history
* Donation statistics
* Volunteer statistics

---

## Notifications

Users receive notifications for important events.

Examples:

* New messages
* Volunteer acceptance
* Delivery updates
* Ratings received
* Post interactions

---

# Long-Term Vision

ShareBridge aims to become a trusted community-driven ecosystem where donors, volunteers, and communities can collaborate efficiently and transparently.

The platform is not only a donation management system but also a social network built around helping others, encouraging trust, accountability, and community participation through technology.
